To understand what brain death is, it's helpful to understand what it's not. Brain death is frequently confused with other states of impaired consciousness, but the biological mechanics are very different:

  

- Coma: The brain is injured but still shows electrical activity on an [EEG](https://www.mayoclinic.org/tests-procedures/eeg/about/pac-20393875). The brainstem is usually intact, and the patient may eventually wake up.

- Persistent Vegetative State (PVS): The "higher brain" (thought and awareness) is gone, but the brainstem (the autopilot) is still functioning. These patients can breathe on their own, have sleep-wake cycles, and may even grimace or cry, though there is no "self" present.

- Brain Death: This is the total "physiological non-existence" of the brain. There is no electrical activity, no blood flow, and no autopilot.
