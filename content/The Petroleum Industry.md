## Thread: The Petroleum Industry

This is where I collect everything related to oil—how we find it, how we use it, and how it runs the world.

### What I’m looking at

Petroleum is more than just fuel for cars. It is the "stuff" that makes almost everything around us. In this thread, I’m taking the industry apart to see:

- **The Science:** How old plants and animals turned into liquid energy over millions of years.

- **The Machines:** How we actually get it out of the ground and turn it into gas, plastic, and medicine.

- **The Impact:** How our need for oil changes where we build cities and how countries treat each other.

### A final thought

Everything from the plastic in your hand to the food on your table usually has a connection to a literal hole in the ground. I hope these notes help you see the invisible pipes that keep our modern world moving.
