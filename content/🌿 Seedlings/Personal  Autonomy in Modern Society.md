Law is intended to establish and maintain societal order, but at what cost to the individual? No doubt, we all appreciate laws that are meant to keep us physically (and otherwise) safe, but laws that govern our bodies, our speech, and by extension, our minds must also be considered. 

**This is a work in progress.** These are my incomplete thoughts while a build a "thinking" framework.
