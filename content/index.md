This is where I sit to watch The Meadow.

My mind doesn’t move in a straight line. I’m a scanner—I follow threads of thought wherever they lead, discovering how one stray observation connects to the next. This site is just a record of those connections.

### How to wander around
This garden is divided into three distinct spaces to help you find your way:
 * 🗺️ [[the meadow/index.md|the meadow]] — The chronological stream of every observation. While it is organized by date, the internal links create bridges across time.
 * 🐝 [The Hive] — Curated collections. This is where I’ve gathered related threads from the Meadow into larger, interconnected themes.
 * 🌑 [The Seed Bank] — The waiting room. This houses raw data, cold leads, and research waiting for a new connection to wake it up.
 
### The Seasons of Thought
As you explore these spaces, you will find ideas in various stages of growth:
 * Pollinating — Fresh sparks I’ve just landed on.
 * Sprouting — Thoughts growing roots and finding their way toward other notes.
 * Honey — Refined thoughts where the connectivity is clear.
 
### Drift with the wind
If you prefer to move without a map, you can catch a stray breeze and land on a [Random Note].
You can also see how this entire ecosystem is stitched together by viewing the [Visual Graph]. It’s the easiest way to see the web of thoughts blooming from this porch.
