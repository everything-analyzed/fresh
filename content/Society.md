### Collective Dynamics

An exploration of how human beings behave in aggregate. This pillar moves away from the individual to look at the patterns, trends, and crises of the group.

- **Active Inquiry:** The Replication Crisis—why do social studies often fail to hold up over time?
    
- **Key Themes:** Sociology, institutional behavior, and the scaling of human interaction.
