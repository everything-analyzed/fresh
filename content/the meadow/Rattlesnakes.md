In the movie *Violent Ends* they basically described a rattlesnake as a lethal killing machines with the patience of a saint. I think I remember hearing somewhere else [[the rattle isn't a threat, but a warning]].

## Thoughts/Questions
- Does a rattlesnake know it's deadly? If so, why wouldn't it just go into immediate attack?

## What I figured out
- Rattlesnakes are actually pretty docile. They do everything they can to stay hidden.
- The rattle is less of a threat and more of a courtesy to let you know it's there so you can keep a respectable distance from each other.
