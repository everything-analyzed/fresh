
## Abstract
  This archive serves as the foundation for my research into the intersection of ego and legal precedent. It tracks the shift from Dr. Kevorkian's early "Mercy Machine" advocacy to the Thomas Youk murder conviction, focusing specifically on how public performance influences legal outcomes.
