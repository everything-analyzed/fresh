 ## Active Inquiry
 To what extent to we truly have personal autonomy?
 
 ## The Proposition
 Law is intended to establish and maintain societal order, but at what cost to the individual? While we appreciate laws for physical safety, we must interrogate those that govern our bodies, speech, and minds.
 <br>
 <br>
 
 ## Open Questions
- **Nature vs. Construct:** Is the necessity of law an inherent human requirement, or a cage we built for ourselves?     
- **Atrophy of Autonomy:** Did society "cripple" its own moral agency by outsourcing it to legal codes? (e.g., the complex ethics of drug legalization).
- **The Kevorkian Case:** How does his advocacy for bodily autonomy highlight the friction between private "freedom" and public "chains"
