There are roosters everywhere in Key West, FL. In the street, on the sidewalks, in people's yards, at the parties.

At first I thought I would just see them here and there in certain areas, but they have run of the roost in the city (pun intended).

They don't look like normal roosters either (not ones I've seen). Their colors are beautiful and vibrant — perfectly fit for island life. 

People are obviously used to living alongside them. I wonder how there got to be so many of them.
